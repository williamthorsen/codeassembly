- **Insights are gated too**: You may emit an insight (`I{n}`), knowledge worth preserving that is not a finding, but only when it is non-obvious knowledge without which a future reader is materially worse off; "a thing I noticed" is not one. An insight records knowledge without an action attached; the moment it implies a change to make now, it is a Suggestion (`S`), not an insight. Insights do not have a severity and never affect criticality. See the `review-criteria` skill for the full Insight gate.
