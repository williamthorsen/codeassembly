# Recommended

The public collection. Membership claims an artifact was examined and found generally applicable: it names nothing specific to one author's environment, states no personal doctrine, declares its prerequisites where a reader looks before invoking, and deploys only where it works.

Membership is per-artifact and enumerated in full rather than by dependency root, so the closure check reads a set nobody's edges can quietly extend.

The skills hold their standing on different grounds. A rulebook of the personal collection reaches `capture-feedback` through a body token, and `capture-feedback` reaches `capture-event` through another; no collection can be closed over an artifact of lesser standing, so the chain pulled both up. `capture-event`'s promotion was forced by that chain rather than chosen on its own merits, so it is the one member to re-examine first. `capture-feedback` holds membership on its own terms: it names no store, declares the registry a capture needs, and routes a record by a rule any registry can answer.

`understanding-codeassembly` holds membership on its own terms too: what it states is a mechanism every consumer meets, not a preference, and the guidance a reader needs beyond the trigger lives in the reference it links rather than in the ambient body every session carries.
