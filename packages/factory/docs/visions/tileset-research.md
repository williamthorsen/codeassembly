## Recommendations

### Best overall for factory visualization (top-down)

1. **sierrassets Automation Tileset** -- Best balance of price (free), quality, and factory specificity. The animated conveyor belts and 6 machine types are exactly what's needed for a factory floor. Top-down perspective matches typical facility visualization.

2. **Elthen Factory Automation Pack** -- The most complete and polished option. Worth the $20 for the depth of content (ores, products, conveyors, pipes, monorail). Top-down perspective is ideal.

3. **DithArt Sci-Fi Tileset** -- Best for a sci-fi facility/space station aesthetic. The modular tileset series lets you mix themes. Try the free version first.

### Best free / CC0 options

1. **OGA Factory Tileset** -- CC0, includes conveyors, pipes, screens. Compact but directly relevant.
2. **0x72 Industrial Tileset** -- CC0, dark industrial mood, good for prototyping.
3. **Futuristic Industrial Technical Tileset** -- CC0, sci-fi themed.

### Best for atmosphere / backgrounds

1. **CraftPix Factory Cyberpunk** -- Free, excellent parallax backgrounds and industrial skylines.
2. **Atomic Realm Industrial** -- 1,250+ tiles with 8 theme variants, strong cyber-industrial aesthetic.

### Suggested approach

For the factory visualization in Excalibur.js, consider a layered approach:

- **Primary tileset:** sierrassets Automation Tileset (32x32, top-down, free) for the core factory floor with conveyors and machines
- **Supplement with:** OGA Factory Tileset (CC0) for additional props like screens and computers
- **If budget allows:** Elthen Factory Automation Pack ($20) as a single comprehensive solution
- **For sci-fi polish:** DithArt's free sci-fi tileset to evaluate the aesthetic

The top-down perspective tilesets (sierrassets, Elthen, DithArt) are most directly applicable to a facility visualization. The side-view/platformer tilesets (Atomic Realm, CraftPix) are better suited for backgrounds or individual prop extraction.
